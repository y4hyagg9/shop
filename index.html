Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ArcNexus.gg

How does ArcNexus.gg protect my purchase?
+

We use a secure escrow system. Your payment is held safely until you confirm delivery of your purchase. If something goes wrong, our 24/7 support team will mediate the dispute and issue a full refund if the seller fails to deliver.

How do I become a seller on ArcNexus.gg?
+

Register for a free account, complete our quick identity verification process, and list your first item in under 5 minutes. Sellers keep up to 95% of each sale after our small platform fee. No subscription required.

What payment methods are accepted?
+

We accept credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT). Additional regional payment methods may be available depending on your location.

How long does delivery take?
+

Most digital goods and resources are delivered instantly or within 15 minutes. Services and custom orders may take up to 24 hours. Each listing clearly states the estimated delivery time before you purchase.

Is ArcNexus.gg safe and legitimate?
+

Absolutely. ArcNexus.gg is a fully legitimate marketplace operating since 2022. All sellers are verified, all transactions are escrow-protected, and we maintain a 99% dispute resolution rate. Over 50,000 gamers trust us daily.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?
+

Yes. If an item is not as described or delivery fails, you can open a dispute within 72 hours of purchase. Our team will review the evidence and issue a full refund if the seller is at fault. Buyer protection is our top priority.
